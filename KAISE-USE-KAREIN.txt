=====================================================
          CLIPING TOOL BY JAY - KAISE USE KAREIN
=====================================================

Yeh tool lambi videos (YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Google Drive ya local PC/D: drive MP4) 
ko auto-captioned 9:16 vertical clips (Shorts/Reels/TikTok) mein convert karta hai.
Yeh 100% free hai aur aapke PC par hi offline chalta hai.

-----------------------------------------------------
1. TOOL CHALU KAISE KAREIN (SILENT LAUNCHER):
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Desktop par "Cliping Tool By JAY" shortcut par double-click karein, ya fir
   folder ke andar "Start-Cliping-Tool-By-JAY.vbs" (ya .bat) par double-click karein.
2. Ab koi bhi Black Terminal/Console window nahi khulegi! Tool background mein 
   silent start hoga aur seedha aapke Chrome/Browser mein khul jayega:
   http://127.0.0.1:8770

-----------------------------------------------------
2. GOOGLE DRIVE SE VIDEO CLIPPING:
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Google Drive link (e.g. https://drive.google.com/file/d/.../view) ko 
   URL box mein paste karein.
2. "⚡ Make clips" button dabayein.
3. Tool apne aap Google Drive se video download karke vertical clips bana dega!

-----------------------------------------------------
3. APNI PC / D: DRIVE KI VIDEOS YA POORA FOLDER BATCH CLIP KAREIN:
-----------------------------------------------------
Agar aapke paas apni videos D: drive ya PC mein download hain:
Tarika A: "📂 Select Video Folder" par click karein aur poora folder select karein.
Tarika B: "Select local video or folder" box mein apna path likhein (e.g. D:\Videos ya D:\Videos\gameplay.mp4)
          aur "🔍 Scan Local Folder / File" button dabayein.
1. Batch Video Queue mein sabhi videos list ho jayengi.
2. "⚡ Start Batch Clipping" button dabayein.
3. Screen par LIVE PROGRESS dikhega:
   - "Processing Video 1 of 5: [video-name]"
   - Overall Batch Progress Bar (%)
   - Current Video Analysis & Rendering Bar (%)
   - Live Clips Created Counter
   - Banate hi sabhi clips real-time screen par aati jayengi!

-----------------------------------------------------
4. CLIPS DOWNLOAD AUR DELETE KAREIN:
-----------------------------------------------------
- Clip ke paas "Download MP4" ya "SRT" se single clip save karein.
- Checkbox se clips tick karke "📥 Download Selected" ya "📦 Download All as ZIP" karein.
- Kisi clip ko hatane ke liye "🗑️ Delete" ya multiple select karke "🗑️ Delete Selected" karein.

-----------------------------------------------------
5. UNLIMITED CLIPS AUR 3 SE 10 MINUTES KI LENGTH:
-----------------------------------------------------
- Clip Count: Ab aap 12 clips tak restricted nahi hain! 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200 ya "Unlimited (500)" button dabayein, ya box mein manchaha number likhein.
- Clip Length: Ab 30s, 1 min (60s), 2 min ke sath-sath 3 min, 4 min, 5 min, 6 min, 7 min, 8 min, 9 min aur 10 minutes (600s) ki clips ka full support hai!
- Ek click mein quick chip (jaise 3 min, 5 min, ya 10 min) select karke clip banayein.

-----------------------------------------------------
6. ULTRA-FAST RENDERING AUR HARDWARE ACCELERATION:
-----------------------------------------------------
- Intel Iris Xe Quick Sync (QSV) Hardware Acceleration ab active hai! Video rendering CPU ki jagah hardware GPU se hoti hai jo 5x-8x tezz hai.
- Caption Accuracy Options:
  1. "⚡ Ultra-Fast: Base Model (Fastest, ~10x speedup)" - Subtitles 2-3 seconds mein ban jaate hain.
  2. "⚡ Balanced: Small Model (High accuracy)" - High accuracy for Hindi/English gaming videos.
  3. "🔬 Studio Hindi: large-v3" - Deep model for difficult Hindi speech.
- Subtitles Toggle: Agar aapko captions nahi chahiye to "Burn in subtitles" ko uncheck karein, clips bina kisi delay ke instant render ho jayengi!
- Parallel Processing: Ab 2 clips ek sath parallel render hoti hain jisse multi-clip export double tezz ho jata hai.
